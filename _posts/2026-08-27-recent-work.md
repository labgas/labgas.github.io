on the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).

## Butyrate and fear extinction memory

*Molecular Psychiatry*, 2026 — **The HDAC inhibitor sodium butyrate moderately enhances
long-term fear extinction memory retrieval in humans.**

Part of the [MoodBugs]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) programme, testing whether a
short-chain fatty acid produced by gut bacteria acts as an epigenetic modulator of fear memory in
humans. It follows earlier work showing that colonic butyrate administration modulates fear
memory without altering the acute stress response, and that variability in threat processing
tracks endogenous butyrate levels.

## Interoception across organ domains

*Biological Psychology*, 2026 — **Comparison of interoceptive abilities across the respiratory,
esophageal and cardiac domain: a pilot study.**

Interoceptive accuracy is often treated as a single trait, largely because it is usually measured
in one modality — typically the heart. This pilot compares abilities across three domains in the
same participants, which is the design needed to ask whether interoception is one ability or
several.

## Representations in the insula

*Nature Communications*, 2026 — **Convergent and selective representations of pain, appetitive
processes, aversive processes, and cognitive control in the insula.**

A collaboration using multivariate approaches to ask what the insula — a region implicated in
almost everything, and therefore explained by almost nothing — actually represents distinctly
versus in common across pain, appetite, aversion and cognitive control.
