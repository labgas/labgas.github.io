## Take part in a study

We regularly recruit **healthy volunteers** and **patients** for studies involving brain imaging,
nutritional or microbial interventions, and psychophysiological measurement. Participation is
always voluntary, informed, and approved by the relevant ethics committee; you can withdraw at
any point.

Studies vary widely in what they ask of participants — some are a single session, others involve
several visits, dietary periods, or MRI and PET scanning. What is involved, and any compensation,
is explained in full before you agree to anything.

To hear about current opportunities, write to our research coordinator
[Liene Bervoets](mailto:liene.bervoets@kuleuven.be) with a short note about yourself.

## Master's and medical students

We host master's and medical students for research internships and thesis projects across all
three research lines. Projects can be experimental (running participants, collecting imaging or
psychophysiological data) or analytical (working with existing datasets).

Get in touch well ahead of your programme's deadlines, and say which research line interests you
and what methods you would like to learn.

## PhD candidates

PhD positions arise through funded projects and through personal fellowship applications —
principally [FWO](https://www.fwo.be/) doctoral fellowships, for which we support strong
candidates through the application process. Deadlines are typically well in advance of the start
date, so early contact matters.

A competitive application usually has a background in one of: medicine, biomedical sciences,
psychology, nutrition, neuroscience, bioengineering, or a quantitative discipline with an
interest in human physiology. Programming experience is valuable but not required at the outset;
our analyses use MATLAB, R, SAS and Python.

## Post-doctoral researchers

Post-doctoral positions arise on funded projects and via personal fellowships (FWO junior and
senior post-doctoral mandates, Marie Skłodowska-Curie actions, and similar). If your work
connects to gut-brain signalling, interoception, appetite regulation, or the microbiota's
influence on affect and cognition, we are glad to discuss a fellowship application.
